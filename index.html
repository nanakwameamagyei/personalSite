How I work

I am a data, AI, and systems researcher with strong applied experience. My work focuses on how data and AI are actually produced, governed, and used inside organisations — not how they are imagined in strategy documents.

I specialise in situations where technical systems meet human work, institutional constraints, and regulation. That includes public sector, research-intensive organisations, and companies dealing with scale, compliance, or long-term sustainability.

Practically, this means I:

  • Map data flows, stakeholders, and constraints
  • Translate organisational needs into technical and governance requirements
  • Prototype or support implementation where needed
  • Bridge technical, managerial, and policy perspectives

I work best in environments that value systems thinking, learning, and long-term impact over quick technical fixes.

Data Curation as Data Governance in a European Research Infrastructure

A four-year qualitative case study within a distributed European environmental research infrastructure (eLTER), examining how data governance is enacted through everyday curation work rather than formal policy alone.

The case reveals why governance fails when human work is invisible — and how polycentric, practice-oriented approaches better support Open Data and FAIR principles at scale.
